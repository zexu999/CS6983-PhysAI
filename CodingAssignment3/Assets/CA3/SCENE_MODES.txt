# Quick scene modes (Inspector)

## After importing your Menagerie arm
- Implement and wire ArmAgent for your MJCF joint/actuator/EE names
- Fill ArmJointController homeTargets + jointRanges from the MJCF

## Collect rollout
- ArmJointController: emitActions=true (random joint goals)
- Disable: WorldModelController, IkGoalProvider
- Enable: RolloutRecorder
- Optional: disable WorldModelDriver

## Live tracking (after training)
- Create Sphere "TrackingTarget" → wire into your IkGoalProvider
- Enable IkGoalProvider + WorldModelController (controller.json)
- ArmJointController: emitActions=false (goals come from IK)
- Disable RolloutRecorder
